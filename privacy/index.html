Privacy policy

This policy explains how GLOWPREY handles information when you use the mobile game and this official website. GLOWPREY is published by MD Apps Labs. The game does not require a player account.

Information handled by the game

Game progression, settings, earned rewards and purchase entitlements are stored locally on your device. GLOWPREY does not operate its own player-profile or gameplay analytics server. We do not ask for your name, postal address, contacts, photographs or precise location in the game.

Advertising and consent

GLOWPREY uses Google AdMob to provide interstitial and optional rewarded advertising. Depending on your region and consent choice, Google and its approved advertising partners may process device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, approximate location and interactions with advertisements.

The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to collect and remember applicable privacy choices before requesting ads. On iOS, App Tracking Transparency is requested when required before access to the advertising identifier. You can revisit available privacy choices from the Privacy Options entry inside the game. Refusing personalized advertising does not block gameplay; the advertising shown may instead be non-personalized or limited. Read Google’s privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. GLOWPREY receives purchase status and product-entitlement information but does not receive or store your complete payment-card details. Store transactions are governed by the privacy terms of the platform through which you purchased.

Retention and control

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you reset the game or remove the application. Advertising and store providers retain data according to their own policies and legal obligations. Where applicable, you may refuse personalized advertising, withdraw consent and manage available privacy choices from within GLOWPREY. Removing the application clears its locally stored gameplay data; purchases remain associated with the Apple or Google account so eligible purchases can be restored.

Service providers and safeguards

GLOWPREY relies on Google for advertising and consent management, and on Apple or Google for store purchases. These providers process data under their published privacy terms and applicable data-protection law. MD Apps Labs does not sell player data and does not give these providers permission to use game data beyond providing, securing and measuring their services.

Children

GLOWPREY is not specifically directed to children below the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. A parent or guardian should supervise use where required by local law.

Your rights and contact

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, object to or delete personal data. For privacy or support requests, visit the official support page or email mdapps.labs@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when GLOWPREY adds services or when legal requirements change. The effective date shown above identifies the latest published version.
